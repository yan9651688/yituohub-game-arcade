# Third-party notices

This is an unofficial fan recreation. Plants vs. Zombies and related names and characters remain associated with their respective rights holders. No affiliation or endorsement is implied. No original game code, music, extracted textures or original game models are included.

The independent procedural 3D geometry from v0.6 was rendered offline with Blender to produce the bundled Canvas 2D sprite atlases. Manifest provenance records the original renderer SHA-256. Version 0.8 supplies distinct procedural models for melon-pult, winter-melon, sea-shroom, dancing zombie and the mechanical boss. Some remaining species retain related simplified source geometry.

The 49 card portraits are newly generated artwork. Their generation records are in assets/cards/provenance.json. They are not extracted original-game textures.
